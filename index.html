About Me

I'm Pavan Sai Kudupudi — a passionate ROS Developer and Automation Engineer currently working full-time at Source Integreta, Noida. My journey in robotics started with a deep curiosity about how machines can perceive and interact with the physical world.

I specialize in building autonomous systems — from humanoid social navigation to lunar rovers — combining cutting-edge algorithms like DR-SPAAM and YOLOv5 with robust sensor fusion pipelines using LiDAR and depth cameras. I thrive where control theory meets real-time software.

My engineering foundation comes from my B.Tech in Automation & Robotics at Amrita Viswa Vidyapeetham, where I also actively participated in national-level competitions, placing 14th in the ISRO Robotics Challenge and 5th in Cisco's Cohort 6 across India.
